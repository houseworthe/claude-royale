## Card Roles & Strategy

### WIN CONDITION: Hog Rider (Slot 6)

**CRITICAL RULE: HOG RIDER IS ALWAYS PLAYED AT THE BRIDGE (ROW E). NO EXCEPTIONS!**

- **Role:** Fast win condition, targets buildings only
- **Placement:** ALWAYS `2E` (left bridge) or `5E` (right bridge) - NO OTHER POSITIONS!
- **Cost:** 4 elixir - cheap enough to cycle quickly
- **Visual:** Brown laughing man with mohawk riding a hog
- **Key Rule:** BRIDGE ONLY at columns 2 or 5. Never play Hog anywhere else!

**Hog Placements (MEMORIZE):**
- `2E` = Left bridge attack
- `5E` = Right bridge attack
- Pick the lane with the weaker tower or less defense
- NO EXCEPTIONS - always 2E or 5E!

### TANK KILLER: Mini P.E.K.K.A (Slot 1)
- **Role:** Destroy high-HP units (Giant, Hog Rider, Knight, Valkyrie)
- **Placement:** Center, 4 tiles from river (row F)
- **Key Rule:** Target tanks, not swarms
- **Anti-Pattern:** Don't use on swarms - gets surrounded

### GROUND SPLASH: Bomber (Slot 2)
- **Role:** Destroy ground swarms (Skeleton Army, Goblins, Barbarians)
- **Placement:** Behind other troops, never alone
- **Weakness:** Dies to any air unit, dies to spells
- **Key Rule:** DEFENSE ONLY - never play alone

### AIR DEFENSE: Mega Minion (Slot 3)
- **Role:** Kill air units (Balloon, Baby Dragon, Minions)
- **Placement:** Reactive - place where air threat is
- **Key Rule:** DEFENSE ONLY - single target

### BUILDING: Tombstone (Slot 4)
- **Role:** Pull and distract enemy troops, spawns skeletons
- **Optimal Placement:** 4-2 position (4 tiles from river, 2 tiles from center)
- **Grid Position:** `4F` or `5F` for center pull
- **Key Rule:** Place BEFORE enemy crosses river, not after

### RANGED DPS: Archers (Slot 5)
- **Role:** Consistent ranged damage, light air defense
- **Placement:** Behind tanks or for defensive support
- **Key Rule:** DEFENSE ONLY - too fragile for solo offense

### CYCLE CARD: Fire Spirit (Slot 7)
- **Role:** Cheap cycle, splash damage vs swarms
- **Cost:** 1 elixir - fastest cycle card
- **Level 11:** Huge advantage, one-shots many units
- **Key Rule:** Use to cycle back to Hog Rider quickly

### SPLASH SUPPORT: Witch (Slot 8)
- **Role:** Splash damage + air defense + skeleton spawns
- **Placement:** Behind Hog on offense, or defensive splash
- **Key Strength:** Hits air AND ground with splash, spawns skeletons continuously
- **Skeleton Value:** Spawned skeletons distract enemy troops and add chip damage

## Hog Cycle Strategy

### OFFENSE: Hog + Support
1. **Basic Hog:** Just Hog Rider at bridge (3E or 6E) - 4 elixir
2. **Hog + Fire Spirit:** Hog at bridge + Fire Spirit behind - 5 elixir, clears swarms
3. **Hog + Witch:** Big push, Hog tanks while Witch splashes + spawns skeletons - 9 elixir

### DEFENSE: Counter then Hog
1. Defend with Mini P.E.K.K.A, Mega Minion, Tombstone, etc.
2. After defense, drop Hog at bridge while opponent is low on elixir
3. **Counter-push timing is key!**

### CYCLE PRIORITY
With 3.1 avg elixir, you can cycle back to Hog Rider fast:
- Play Fire Spirit (1) + Archers (3) + Bomber (2) = 6 elixir to cycle
- This gets you back to Hog quickly after playing it

## Matchup Notes

### vs Minion Horde
- Witch splashes them down
- Fire Spirit also clears them (Level 11 advantage)

### vs Giant Pushes
- Mini P.E.K.K.A on the Giant
- Tombstone to pull and distract
- Counter with Hog after defense

### vs Other Hog Riders
- Tombstone pull (place before Hog crosses)
- Mini P.E.K.K.A if Tombstone not in hand

### vs Swarms (Skeleton Army, Goblins)
- Fire Spirit clears fast
- Bomber from behind
- Witch splash
